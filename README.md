# Market Pulse

A local, live market dashboard: S&P 500 heatmap, money flows, breadth, fear & greed, global markets, rates, crypto, commodities, FX and news, all on one dark-terminal page.

## Run it

Double-click **`start.bat`**. It installs dependencies the first time, starts the server and opens <http://localhost:3000>.

Or from a terminal:

```bash
npm install
npm start
```

Close the console window (or press Ctrl+C) to stop it. Run locally, the server only listens on `127.0.0.1`, so only this PC can open it.

## On a phone

At 767px wide and below the page switches to a phone layout: a bottom tab bar (**Market, Flows, Stocks, World, News**) shows one group of panels at a time. The tab you were on is remembered, tapping the active tab scrolls back to the top, the World tab has chips that jump to each section, and a stock's chart opens as a full-screen sheet that the phone's back gesture closes. Wider screens keep the full single-page dashboard.

## What's on the page

| Section | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Ticker strip + futures | Major indices, VIX, 10Y yield, dollar, gold, oil, bitcoin, plus index futures (which keep trading overnight) |
| Market regime bar | One-line read: drawdown, advancers/decliners, equal-weight move, fear score, risk appetite |
| S&P 500 heatmap | Size = market cap. Color modes: **Perf** (1D–1Y), **Ext hrs** (pre/after-market), **Rel vol** (unusual activity), **Off high** (damage from the 52-week high). Click a sector name to zoom. |
| Fear & Greed | Composite of 7 signals, plus how the 6 signals with daily history read yesterday, a week, a month and a year ago, with a 1-year chart |
| Market Brief | Plain-English notes generated by rules, including "worst day since…", failed breakouts, sector rotation, credit stress, safe-haven demand |
| Money flow | Sector performance, a sector rotation chart, a risk-on/off scoreboard, asset-class flows with relative volume, and index-point contributors |
| Breadth & movers | Advance/decline, up/down volume, % above 50/200-day averages, distribution of moves, 1-year breadth trend, movers, volatility term structure |
| Earnings | S&P 500 reports in the next 7 days and the reaction to recent reports |
| Watchlist | Any ticker (saved in your browser). Use ☆ in a chart to add it. |
| Global / Rates / Crypto / Commodities & FX / News | World map, Treasury yield curve and spreads, crypto heatmap, futures and currencies, live headlines |

Press **/** anywhere to search any ticker (stocks, ETFs, futures, FX, crypto) and open its chart, which shows 50/200-day averages, key stats and related news.

## Data sources

- Yahoo Finance (no API key). Refreshes quotes every 15 s while the US market is open and every 45 s otherwise. Some exchanges are delayed up to 15 minutes.
- U.S. Treasury daily par yield curve.
- S&P 500 constituents from the open `datasets/s-and-p-500-companies` list (cached in `data/`, refreshed weekly).

The Fear & Greed score, risk score and market brief are simple local rules for context. They are not investment advice.

## Files

- `server.js` runs the HTTP server, the refresh loops and the live stream (Server-Sent Events).
- `src/` holds the Yahoo client, the symbol universe (`config.js`) and the analytics (`engine.js`).
- `public/` holds the page (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`). `js/tabs.js` runs the phone tab bar.
- Optional environment variables: `PORT` (default 3000), `HOST` (listen address, default `127.0.0.1`), `SITE_PASSWORD` (ask visitors for a password).
